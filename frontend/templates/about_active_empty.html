Do you have a book you love so much you'd like to give it to the world? At Unglue.it, you can.

We want everyone to be able to read beloved books -- for free, on their favorite devices, worldwide. We also want the book creators to earn a living. Traditionally, readers pay every time they buy a book. But what if it worked more like public radio? At Unglue.it, creators set their books free under Creative Commons licenses, and people everywhere chip in to support them -- people like you.

If 's favorite books had ungluing campaigns running right now, you'd see them here. Every time an Ungluer like supports a campaign with a contribution, they reward the creator for making the book free for everyone to read and share.
